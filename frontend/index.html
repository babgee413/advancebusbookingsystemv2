About the ATBU Shuttle Service

The Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University (ATBU), Bauchi operates an inter-campus shuttle service connecting the Yelwa Campus and Gubi Campus.

Our online booking system allows registered students to purchase bus tickets in advance, eliminating the uncertainty of the traditional boarding process. Each bus has a maximum capacity of 12 passengers — once a bus is fully booked, it is marked as unavailable.

Register with your ATBU registration number, log in, select a trip, pay for your ticket, and receive your digital bus ticket. It's that simple.

Travel Policy

Please review these important guidelines before booking your ticket.

⏰ Arrive Early

Please arrive at the bus stop at least 15 minutes before the scheduled departure time to ensure timely boarding.

🚫 Missed Bus = Non-Refundable

If you miss your scheduled bus, your ticket and payment are not refundable. Plan your arrival accordingly.

🎫 Bring Your Ticket

Present your printed or digital ticket to the bus conductor before boarding. Both formats are accepted.

🪪 Valid ID Required

Carry your ATBU student ID along with your ticket for verification at the boarding point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the shuttle booking system.

How do I register for an account? ▼
Click "Register Now" on the homepage. Fill in your full name, ATBU registration number (format: NN/NNNNNa/N), email, phone number, and a password. You'll be able to log in immediately after registration.
What payment methods are accepted? ▼
We accept debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and mobile wallet payments. All transactions are processed securely through our payment gateway.
Can I cancel my booking and get a refund? ▼
Refunds are only available for cancellations made before the bus departs. Once you miss the bus, no refunds will be issued. Please contact support for cancellation requests.
What happens if the bus is full? ▼
Each bus has a maximum capacity of 12 passengers. Once a bus is fully booked, it will be marked as "Fully Booked" and you'll need to select a different departure time or date.
How do I view my ticket? ▼
After payment, you'll be redirected to your ticket page automatically. You can also find all your tickets in the "My Tickets" section of your dashboard. Download as PDF or print directly.
